About Me

I’m Andrew Smith, a software developer with over a decade of experience producing full-stack web applications in a range of industries. I’ve spent my developer career working in small teams in a mix of permanent and contractor roles, all requiring the growth of traits in responsibility, clear communication, and adaptability. I am at my professional best when entrusted with large, autonomous projects focused on serving users with new capabilities or in improving software systems in measurable ways. This could mean new features to delight customers, or making formerly clunky tooling easy and intuitive for other members of the development team. I would gladly speak to any interested parties looking for an employee, contractor, or consultant who is eager to bring these traits and experiences to work for them.
